What NEXORA does

Finding the right hackathon, internship or workshop usually means hours of searching across many websites. NEXORA does that search for you. Tell us about your skills and interests once, and we show the opportunities you are actually eligible for, with a clear reason for each one.

How it works

1. Build your profile

Add your course, skills, projects and interests. Update it any time you learn something new.

2. Get matched

NEXORA compares your profile with each opportunity and labels it Excellent Match, Good Match, Beginner Friendly or Skill Development.

3. Grow step by step

If something is out of reach, you see exactly what is missing and what to learn next.
